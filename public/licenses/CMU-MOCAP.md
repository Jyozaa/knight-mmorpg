# CMU motion capture provenance and use terms

The v0.5 game includes joint curves retargeted to its original knight from CMU Graphics Lab subject 16: take 15 (straight walk), 35 (run/jog), 17 (walking turn), 08 (run/stop), and 33 (slow walk/stop). These are full-body human performance captures, sampled originally at 120 Hz.

The official site permits incorporation into commercial products and its FAQ permits copying, modification and redistribution. It prohibits reselling the motion data itself, including converted data. These curves are incorporated into this game; they are not offered as a motion asset pack. This permission is not CC0 and should not be described as such.

Sources checked 17 November 2026:

- https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/
- https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/faqs.php
- https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/search.php?subjectnumber=16
