# Grass and movement audio

Creator: Kenney (https://kenney.nl).
Pack: Impact Sounds 1.0, https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds.
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.
Verified against the creator's pack page on 17 November 2026. The legal code is included as `Kenney-CC0.txt`.

The creator's download endpoint redirected to an HTTP 403 response. Identical named OGG assets were obtained from the public mirror https://github.com/Mcamento8/open-game-sfx-index/tree/main/audio/impact-sounds. File hashes are recorded in `grass-audio-sha256.json`.

Bundled files: five `footstep_grass` variations, three `footstep_carpet` variations, three `impactSoft_heavy` variations and three `impactMetal_light` variations. Original OGG payloads are unmodified. Carpet samples provide a quiet dry/soft friction layer; soft impacts supply boot weight, and metal impacts provide very quiet armor response. Grass-sample tails are crossfaded in memory into a wind-driven vegetation bed and used for leg brushing. These are layered sample assets, not runtime oscillator/noise footsteps. The original recording technique was not independently established.
